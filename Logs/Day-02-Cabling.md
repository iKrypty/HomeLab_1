Day 2: 10/11/2026

Goal: To learn how to terminate, crimp, and make RJ45 connections.
  * Followed a YT tutorial on how to crimp and how to use the crimping tool properly.
  * Took an hour and a half to fully and successfully crimp 1 side properly using the boot & the plug.
  * Watching the video taught me what the different networking tools are, how they work, when they are needed and proper use of them.

What I learned:
  * Following the T568B Order, I fully memorized the order after crimping 3 or 4 times.
    * White orange, orange, white green, blue, white blue, green, white brown, brown.
  * After crimping another cable on the following day, I found it a lot easier to insert the cables into the plug by stripping the jacket an inch or more away from the end leaving me more than enough slack on the cables to line up and insert.
  * Creating small patch cables with bulk cat6 cables was not ideal at all.
  * Went ahead and ordered a 10 pack premium ultra thin braided 0.15m patch cables to connect my Pi to Switch, and leave some extra for future use.
  * I do need to mention the importance of cable management here due to the different sizes of power adapters taking up considerable space.
